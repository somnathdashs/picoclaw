# Built-in Skills

PicoClaw includes ready-to-use built-in skills that extend the assistant's capabilities for specialized tasks.

---

### Handle Any PDF Works (`handle-any-pdf-works`)

The **Handle Any PDF Works** skill gives PicoClaw complete tools to work with PDF documents:

- **Reading & Extraction**: Extracts readable text and structured tables from digital PDFs.
- **OCR for Scans**: Automatically runs optical character recognition (OCR) on scanned documents, low-contrast pages, or multilingual files.
- **Vision Fallback**: Uses image-based reading when documents contain complex layouts, handwriting, stamps, or uncommon scripts.
- **Organization**: Merges multiple PDF files into one, splits large files into individual pages, or rotates sideways pages.
- **Creation & Forms**: Builds brand new PDF documents, fills PDF forms, and applies watermarks or password protection.

#### How to Use
Whenever you upload or mention a `.pdf` file in chat, PicoClaw automatically uses this skill to inspect, extract, or edit your document.
